# Visual style profiles

Use one profile as the semantic grammar for the complete cover set. All three profiles inherit `scene_to_white_micro_3d`: a recognizable real scene naturally grows into a white-material micro-3D explanatory structure rather than becoming a split-screen collage.

## Shared construction rules

- Give one absolute subject the largest visual weight.
- Use a real action in progress, not a static pose or decorative still life.
- Let one person, object, surface, route, interface, product, or flow cross the 10% to 15% transition zone.
- Continue the same light direction, color temperature, camera height, vanishing point, highlights, contact shadows, and ambient occlusion.
- Simplify real materials progressively into white micro-3D blocks, layers, nodes, paths, cutaways, or containers.
- Give the real human scene and the white-material information outline exactly half of the canvas each; never let the information structure become denser than the human story.
- Require a visible person with a readable face or hands, a clear role, and an unfinished action. Make the person act out the title's physical metaphor; a person merely looking at a screen is not enough.
- Make the scene observationally real: credible location, scale, body or object state, ambient light, surface wear, incidental traces, and an action caught in progress. Avoid studio posing, symbolic scenery, polished advertising sets, and generic stock-photo perfection.
- Use white material as a concise information field; use accent colors only to encode the one key state, relationship, risk, choice, or outcome.
- Limit the information outline to two to four large nodes, one directional relationship, and one conclusion. It must be understandable as a thumbnail, not complete enough to replace the video.
- Use 5 to 8 purposeful visual elements across the complete cover. Reserve texture and incidental detail for the real scene; keep information components large and sparse.
- Keep Chinese labels short and physically attached to a card, tab, plate, screen, or pointer.
- Use zero to three Chinese labels. Avoid small annotations, secondary branches, legends, axes, dense data, hard dividers, unrelated halves, flat flowcharts, dashboards, floating icon clouds, meaningless arrows, and generic AI spectacle.

## `pearl_mist_midlife`

Use for relationships, life choices, personal experience, wellbeing, family, career transitions, and reflective stories.

### Subject logic

- Use a person who credibly mirrors the source story and performs its central metaphorical action. Do not automatically force the source skill's female lead when the user's video centers another identity.
- When a woman around age 30 is appropriate, preserve the source design: naturally attractive, intellectually composed, real skin, coordinated features, clear gaze, restrained clothing, and a non-performative unfinished action.
- Express uncertainty, restraint, regret, realization, or grounded relief without victim imagery, exaggerated despair, glamour advertising, sexualization, or influencer-template faces.
- Make the information outline summarize only the most important causality, time contrast, choice, relationship, or realistic path forward.

### Palette

- Background: pearl mist `#F3F4F1`, a warm-gray neutral white with tonal depth rather than blank pure white.
- Primary accent: rain blue `#526A7A` for judgment and depth.
- Supporting: warm copper `#B67850` for lived warmth; dark wine `#874F52` only for conflict or cost; gray green `#718274` for repair, order, and action space.
- Preserve a mature life-magazine feel using warm-gray surfaces, translucent layers, soft environmental shadows, and realistic household or workplace materials.

### Information forms

Use `causal_chain`, `before_after`, `timeline`, `layers`, `choice_path`, or `relationship_map`.

## `cool_porcelain_tech`

Use for software, AI, devices, code, interfaces, workflows, architecture, comparisons, and troubleshooting.

### Subject logic

- Show a credible practitioner actively handling the actual recognizable device, interface, terminal, code state, system component, error, or operation described by the source.
- Let a screen edge, code line, port, data packet, interface, or error state unfold into the explanatory structure.
- Make the information outline summarize only the decisive mechanism, data step, comparison, or repair choice; leave implementation detail to the video.
- Include technical micro-details only when they improve understanding: ports, state lights, code lines, error markers, slots, connectors, equipment edges, and status changes.
- Avoid generic robots, luminous brains, abstract chips, neon cities, random code rain, and invented UI screenshots.

### Palette

- Background: cool porcelain `#EEF3F6` with cold white, light steel gray, frosted glass, translucent plates, and precise contact shadows.
- Primary accent: electric blue `#1677FF` for main action and data flow.
- Supporting: teal `#11A8A5` for success and valid paths; indigo `#6256D9` for branches and abstraction; coral red `#E25B5B` only for errors, blockage, and risk.

### Information forms

Use `mechanism_snapshot`, `architecture_flow`, `data_flow`, `sequence`, `layers`, `comparison`, `lifecycle`, or `troubleshooting_path`.

## `capital_paper_business`

Use for products, customers, companies, monetization, cost, profit, cash, competition, industry structure, and capital expectations.

### Subject logic

- Show a credible customer, operator, founder, worker, or decision maker actively handling a real product, company operation, facility, goods movement, payment evidence, or industrial scene.
- Let a product, receipt, container, device, shipment, customer action, or infrastructure element become the entry to the information layer.
- Make the information outline summarize only the decisive value flow, cost constraint, competitive relationship, key variable, or expectation gap; leave the full model to the video.
- Include business micro-details only when meaningful: scales, tickets, rails, slots, wear, capacity marks, transaction containers, inventory, and facility interfaces.
- Avoid stock-chart piles, abstract rising arrows, glowing finance cities, ungrounded money symbols, and investment recommendations.

### Palette

- Background: capital paper `#F2F1ED`, a neutral warm report-paper white with light graphite surfaces, embossed paper, frosted glass, anodized metal, and clear contact shadows.
- Primary accent: deep navy `#244A5A` for the core business and value path.
- Supporting: graphite `#34383F` for cost and constraint; metallic gold `#B58A4A` for value and scarcity; strategic red `#A55252` only for risk and conflict; calm teal `#4B7C78` for efficiency and improvement.

### Information forms

Use `value_flow`, `cost_structure`, `profit_tree`, `cash_cycle`, `competition_map`, `decision_signal`, or `before_after`.
